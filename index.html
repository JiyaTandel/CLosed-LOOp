    Today's orders

    You are mission control. You never move the astronauts yourself. Each day you decide how they spend their time and where the power goes, then press End day and the outpost lives through 24 hours. Every number you set changes the picture on the left.
    Crew hours
    4 astronauts × 8 h = 32 h is a normal working day. You can order up to 40 h, but anything above 32 h is overtime and builds fatigue. Tired crews get hurt. Press the ? on each line to see what that job does.
    Shelter the crew
    Hide underground today.
    What: the whole crew goes into the underground storm shelter. Why: during a solar storm this cuts the radiation dose to 15%. Cost: nobody can work outside, so no shielding and no panel cleaning today. Do not shelter on quiet days unless you have to.
    Greenhouse lights
    What: brightness of the grow lights, from 0 to 100%. Why: bright lights grow crops that give food and oxygen and absorb CO2. Cost: the lights use a lot of power (up to 120 kWh a day). During the lunar night, when there is no solar power, dimming them saves the battery. Crops left in the dark for 2 days die.
    Power priority
    What: the order in which systems get electricity. Why: when the power supply is short, systems at the top are served first and the ones at the bottom get cut. Tip: keep heat, air and water above the greenhouse. Use Up and Down to re-order.
      End day: locks in your orders and runs one day. Skip ahead: repeats the same orders day after day, and stops by itself when something new appears (an alert, a storm, a breakdown) or after 7 days. Use it when everything is calm, not when trouble is coming.
